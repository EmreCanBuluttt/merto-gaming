FOLD RUNNER is a neon racing game designed exclusively for iPhone Duo: the hinge is the controller. It needs iPhone Duo with iOS 27.1 or later; on other iPhones it only shows a notice that it is designed for iPhone Duo.

How do I play?

Tap START; a short tutorial follows. Swipe left or right to change lanes. Fold the device to change the world: fully open is Open World, about 130° is Vertical Shift (score ×1.5), about 100° is Compression (score ×2). Close the device and the run continues on the cover display in Pocket Mode.

How does Fold Boost work?

Unfold from a folded mode all the way to Open World. Speed doesn't matter and it never needs force. A boost lasts 3 seconds and then recharges.

The screen switched off while I folded quickly

When the device is folded quickly, iOS can move the app to the cover display while it is still open. FOLD RUNNER then pauses the run; open the device fully and tap RESUME.

It stutters or gets warm

Settings → Graphics: switch from High to Medium or Low. Reduced Motion in Settings also removes camera shake and roll.

Are there ads, purchases or accounts?

No. The game works offline, needs no account and collects no data. See the privacy policy.

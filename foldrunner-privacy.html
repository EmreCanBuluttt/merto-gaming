Effective date: 10 November 2026

In short: FOLD RUNNER does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, no accounts and no internet connection.

1. Scope

This policy applies to FOLD RUNNER (App Store name "FOLD RUNNER: Hinge Racer"), a racing game for iPhone Duo published on the App Store by Emre Can Bulut (Merto Gaming).

2. Data we collect

None. The app does not collect names, e-mail addresses, location, device identifiers, advertising IDs, usage statistics or any other personal data, and does not send anything to us or to third parties. The app does not connect to the internet.

3. Information stored only on the device

To make the game work, the following is kept only on your device, in the app's own storage. Nobody, including us, can access it:

  • Game progress: best score, lifetime statistics and completed objectives.
  • Settings: sound and volume, graphics quality, Reduced Motion, and whether the tutorial has been completed.

The game reads the iPhone Duo's hinge angle and posture to play; this is used only while you play and is never stored or sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes everything from the device.

4. Permissions

The app requests no permissions: no camera, photo library, location, microphone, contacts or tracking.

5. Ads, purchases and external links

There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no social sharing, no chat and no links out of the app.

6. Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, it performs no data processing within the scope of the US COPPA, the EU GDPR or Turkey's KVKK (Law No. 6698).

7. Stores and operating systems

The App Store and your device's operating system may process information under their own privacy policies (e.g. download records or crash reports you choose to share with developers). This is outside our control.

8. Changes

If we update this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and update the date. If a change would ever require collecting data, we will announce it clearly in the app in advance.
